Rules

Decisive result: Winner + exact score = 5 · Winner + one exact score = 3 · Winner only = 1.
Draw in 90 (to penalties): Predicted a draw + correct winner = 5 · Predicted a draw = 3 · Winner only (no draw predicted) = 1.
Goal-timing bonus (Quarter Final onwards): for each goal in your predicted scoreline, pick the period it is scored in (1st half / 2nd half / extra time). Earn +3 for each goal whose period you get right, matched per team. Optional, and awarded independently of the score.
